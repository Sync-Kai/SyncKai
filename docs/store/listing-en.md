## Detailed description

```
Your anime list updates itself.

Watch an episode on your platform as usual: as soon as the ending credits start, SyncKai saves your progress to AniList and/or MyAnimeList. No more opening your list after every episode.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

▶ HOW IT WORKS
1. Connect your AniList account, your MyAnimeList account, or both.
2. Start an episode on Crunchyroll, ADN, Netflix, Disney+ or Prime Video.
3. At the credits, a small bubble confirms: "Episode 6 saved". Done.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ NEW IN 2.5
• Sequel alerts, and an "Announced" calendar of what's coming for your series.
• The watch order of the whole franchise, next to the video.
• "For you": series to discover from what you rated highly, read from AniList.
• A new popup with a side rail: Watching, Activity, For you, Schedule and Stats.
• Stats: what SyncKai synced day after day, and your profile statistics.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✦ AUTOMATIC SYNC
• At the start of the ending credits, or at the percentage you choose (70 to 98%).
• The right seasons on the right entries, even with continuous numbering (One Piece) or a season split into several parts.
• When in doubt, nothing is sent blindly: a "To check" card lets you pick the right entry.
• Network drop? The sync is retried automatically.

✦ THE POPUP
• "Watching": your series in progress, the next episode ("Ep. 5 in 18 h"), −/+ in one click, a button to resume on your platform, the airing status and the time left to catch up ("≈ 50 min"). Sort by next release, time left, episodes left or airing status; a button re-reads your list after a change on AniList or MyAnimeList.
• Dormant series: those with no episode watched for 30 days (or 60, 90, never) are grouped at the bottom, to pause, drop or keep in one click.
• "On this page": the AniList entry of the open series or episode, to add it to your list or change progress, status, rating or your note, with your friends on AniList who have it in their list.
• "Activity": the latest syncs, the entries to check and the announced sequels.
• "For you": suggestions from your scores, with genre chips and an All / Series / Movies choice.
• "Schedule": your week of releases and the "Announced" view, as in the side panel.
• "Stats": the episodes synced over 7 days, 30 days, 12 months or all time, your totals, statuses, scores, top genres, formats, studios and decades (on MyAnimeList: statuses, mean score and rewatches).

✦ THE SIDE PANEL
• "Now playing", next to the video: the episode's entry, your live progress ("Sync in 2 min 10"), your list, the episode discussion and "In the same spirit".
• Watch order: seasons, films, OVAs and side stories in release order, what you watched ticked off, what comes next and a "Watch" button on your platform.
• "Schedule": this week's releases of your series, at the estimated time on your platform; a calendar jumps to any week. The "Announced" view groups the coming sequels and the series of your list not out yet by season, with "+ Plan to watch".
• "Discover": a suggestion at a time, with the reason ("Because you rated … ★ 9/10"), "+ Plan to watch" and "Not interested".
• Open it on any page, from the popup or with Alt+Shift+1.

✦ IMPORT YOUR HISTORY
• Crunchyroll, ADN or Netflix: SyncKai reads your history in your browser, shows you a preview, then updates your list.
• Progress never goes backwards, and every doubt becomes a check instead of a write.

✦ AND ALSO
• New episode alerts, at the release time on your platform, and sequel alerts once a day (browser notification or only in Activity).
• End-of-series rating with 10 stars (half stars included), converted to your AniList score format; rewatches supported.
• AniList ↔ MyAnimeList comparison to spot and align differences, only when you decide.
• Excluded series, Alt+Shift+K to mark the current episode as watched, backup of your settings.
• Interface in 21 languages.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

PLATFORMS
• Crunchyroll and ADN (Animation Digital Network)
• Optional, to turn on in Settings: Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video (anime only, other titles are ignored)

TRACKING SERVICES
• AniList and MyAnimeList, together or separately
• Suggestions, sequels and the watch order are read from AniList's public catalogue

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 PRIVACY
SyncKai has no server. Your settings, history, activity statistics and sign-ins stay in your browser. The extension only reads the current episode page on the supported platforms (Netflix, Disney+ and Prime Video only if you turn them on) and your history only if you start an import. It only talks to AniList and MyAnimeList: to sync, to read your lists once a day for sequel alerts and when suggestions are shown, and to read the public lists of the people you follow on AniList, never stored. Nothing is sent to the developer.
```

## Suggested category

**Lifestyle › Entertainment**

Rationale: the extension is only used while watching anime on streaming sites and keeps a personal watch list up to date — a leisure use case. "Productivity" (tools, workflow, communication) targets work use and would make the listing harder to find for anime fans browsing Entertainment, where streaming-related extensions live.
