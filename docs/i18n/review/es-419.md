# Review log: Spanish (Latin America), `es-419`

Independent review of the `es-419` catalog (`src/i18n/locales/es-419.json`, 934 keys) and of the browser strings (`public/_locales/es_419/messages.json`). The reviewer is a native speaker of Latin American Spanish, reviewing from the point of view of an anime fan in Mexico, Argentina, Colombia or Chile. Date: 2026-10-14.

Binding references: `docs/i18n/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/i18n/glossary/es-419.md`. Every key was read with its note in `docs/i18n/context.json` and compared with `en.json`, `fr.json` (the original language of the product) and `de.json`.

## Procedure

1. **Terminology audit.** Each decision in the term sheet was checked against fan usage and the shared glossary. Each core term was then searched across the whole catalog. All of them are used consistently:
   - series, season, episode, list and entry (*ficha*);
   - the six list statuses, the Watching tab and Activity;
   - sync, to check, to rate, score, comment, match, excluded and pending sync;
   - credits, popup (*ventana emergente*, 6 keys), side panel, Now playing, Schedule and On this page;
   - preferred player, history import, backup, preview and rewatch;
   - account, connect and session expired;
   - release alerts, Settings and its categories.

   No term decision was reversed. Three small conventions were added to the term sheet:
   - no gerund for future events in countdowns;
   - *Semana anterior* / *Semana siguiente* for the Schedule arrows;
   - no echo of *Configuración* in settings paths.
2. **Key-by-key review of all 934 keys.** Each key was checked for:
   - meaning, grammar and naturalness;
   - placeholders, filled with realistic values;
   - composed texts (`{service}: …`, lowercase fragments, relative times after *Analizado*, *Completada* and *Última revisión:*);
   - cross-references to other UI labels;
   - length against the limits in the notes;
   - plurals, rendered for 0, 1, 2, 21 and 101.

   Automatic searches found no leftovers of the following kinds:
   - Spain-only vocabulary (*vale*, *ordenador*, *vídeo*, *icono*, *pulsar*, *ajustes*, *añadir*, *gestionar*, *introducir*, *copia de seguridad*, *Mayús*…);
   - *voseo*, *vosotros* or Spain-style present perfect;
   - « » or straight quotes, three-dot ellipses or `85 %`;
   - English text. Only legitimate cases remain: OK, OVA, Spin-off, Shift, tokens, Redirect URL, and units such as `min`, `h`, `s`, `d`.
3. **Browser strings** (`messages.json`): natural and neutral. `extDescription` has 85 characters (limit 132). The two shortcut descriptions are identical to `settings.shortcuts.episode` and `settings.shortcuts.panel`. No change.
4. **Check**: `npm run i18n:check -- es-419` reports 0 errors and 70 informative length warnings, reviewed below.

## Length warnings reviewed

The 70 warnings of `npm run i18n:check` were all reviewed:
- Most are screen-reader labels (`*Aria`), status lines or headings with room to spare.
- Some are short English words that are simply longer in Spanish: *Guardar*, *Detener*, *Reintentar*, *Configuración*.
- The footer status texts (*3 sincronizaciones pendientes*) fit. The status area of the popup footer is about 236 px for 12 px text, so about 36 characters.
- `agenda.next` (*Semana siguiente*) is new. It is the tooltip and aria-label of a button that shows only an arrow, so it cannot overflow.

Items that need a visual check in the real UI:
- `panel.tab.nowPlaying`;
- `settings.autoSync.label` (*Sincronización automática*, 26 characters for a limit of 25; German is longer);
- `compare.filter.missingAniList` (*Faltan en AniList*, 17 for 16, the same as German).

## Out-of-scope finding (code, not translation)

`src/shared/netflix-import.ts` › `netflixTabOp` finds Netflix's "Show more" button with a fixed list of French, English and German labels: `voir plus`, `afficher plus`, `show more`, `see more`, `load more`, `mehr anzeigen`, `weitere anzeigen`, `mehr laden`.

On a Netflix profile set to Spanish, the button reads *Mostrar más*. It is never clicked, so the import reads only the first page of the viewing activity (about 20 rows). No "partial history" notice is shown: `state.more` is false, so `readNetflixHistory` stops at `if (!state.more) break;` with `partial: false`.

The Netflix display language is set per profile, independently of SyncKai's language. This therefore affects every non-FR/EN/DE Netflix profile. It also contradicts `crImport.netflix.rule`, which tells the user that SyncKai uses the “Mostrar más” button.
