## Task 1: Make the two e2e focus assertions wait for the focus (ARCH-01)

**Branch:** `chore/e2e-focus-waits`
**Findings:** ARCH-01 in `findings.md` › 2.

- Change nothing else. Do not skip, disable or loosen any other test.
