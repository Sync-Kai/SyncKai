## Detailed description

```
SyncKai keeps your anime list up to date on its own. Start an episode on Crunchyroll or ADN: the extension recognizes the series and saves your progress to AniList and/or MyAnimeList at the start of the credits. No manual input.

Features
• Auto sync at the start of the credits (Crunchyroll) or at an adjustable percentage (70–98%).
• AniList and MyAnimeList, together or separately: each account is optional.
• Side panel on Crunchyroll and ADN, "Now playing" tab: the episode's AniList entry (genres, studio, average score), your live progress ("Sync in 2 min 10"), your actions, sequels with a "Watch" button and a link to the episode discussion.
• "Schedule" in the side panel: this week's releases for the series you're watching, with the estimated time on Crunchyroll or ADN (adjustable delay, per series if needed) and the episodes you've already seen ticked off.
• Crunchyroll history import (Settings): SyncKai reads your history in your browser, shows you a preview, then updates AniList and MyAnimeList in the background. It never goes backwards; when in doubt, it asks you to review instead of writing.
• Season matching, including absolute numbering (e.g. One Piece) and seasons split across several entries.
• "To check": when a match is uncertain, you pick the right entry from the popup. Nothing is sent blindly.
• "Watching": your series in progress, the next episode's date ("Ep. 5 in 18 h"), +1 / −1, and "Open" on Crunchyroll or ADN (SyncKai remembers the ADN series pages you visit; otherwise "Search on ADN").
• "On this page": on a Crunchyroll / ADN series or episode page, the AniList entry of what you are watching; add it to your list or change progress, status and score without leaving the page.
• One-click status: put on hold, drop or mark as completed, on AniList and MyAnimeList.
• Compare AniList ↔ MyAnimeList: spots differences (progress, status, score, missing series) and aligns them in one click, never automatically.
• Rating at the end of a series (out of 10, converted to your AniList score format) and rewatch support.
• New episode alerts: a Chrome notification when an episode is out, with an adjustable delay.
• Pending syncs: if the network drops, the sync is retried automatically.
• Keyboard shortcut Alt+Shift+S to mark the current episode as watched.
• Excluded series and on-page notifications (Discreet, Detailed, Alerts only), fullscreen-friendly.
• Settings by category, with a summary of each setting and quick toggles, in the popup and the side panel.
• Backup: export / import your settings, matches and history (never your AniList/MAL sign-ins).
• Diagnostic report (Settings › Help): attach it to a bug report, with no sensitive data.
• Interface in English, French and German.

Supported platforms
• Crunchyroll
• ADN (Animation Digital Network)
• Netflix, optional (Settings › Playback & sync): only anime linked to Netflix on AniList, other titles are ignored

Tracking services
• AniList
• MyAnimeList

Privacy
SyncKai has no server. Your data (settings, matches, history, sign-ins) stays in your browser's local storage. The extension reads the current episode page on Crunchyroll/ADN (and Netflix, only if you turn it on: access to netflix.com is then requested), your Crunchyroll history only if you start the import, and only shares your data with AniList and MyAnimeList. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Source code and issue reports: https://github.com/Sync-Kai/SyncKai
```

## Suggested category

**Lifestyle › Entertainment**

Rationale: the extension is only used while watching anime on streaming sites and keeps a personal watch list up to date — a leisure use case. "Productivity" (tools, workflow, communication) targets work use and would make the listing harder to find for anime fans browsing Entertainment, where streaming-related extensions live.
