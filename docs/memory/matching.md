# Matching

Season and entry resolution, mappings, AniList search and request budget, matching signals. Part of the project memory (`CLAUDE.md` › Project memory).

- **Single entry spanning a multi-season Netflix show** (One Piece arcs): on a series page, `representsWholeShow` (`matching.ts`, the judge's `wholeShow`) makes the single directly linked entry certain when it covers every Netflix season (enough episodes, or airing with no earlier series entry); otherwise « Saison N mais une seule fiche » stays a doubt. UI (`ui/page-media-view.ts` › `seasonPickerOptions`, `ui/components/season-picker.tsx`): no « Saison devinée » warning without a selector (fewer than 2 choices).
- **Crunchyroll arcs with an episode range** (One Piece « Elbaph (1156-current) », also « 1086-1155 », « 1156–actuel », « 1156- »): `parseCrunchyrollSeasonLabel` returns the title and `firstEpisode`, sent as `PageMediaInfo.seasonFirstEpisode` and used as the absolute number in the series-page resolution. Relation propagation (`resolver.ts` › `isSeasonRelation`): a PREQUEL released after its source (or a SEQUEL released before), or an entry linked only to another supported platform, never becomes a season by relation (MONSTERS, ONA 2024, « prequel » of One Piece, Netflix only). The signal groups (`signalGroups`) keep in-order entries linked only elsewhere on purpose (audit 1-D5, tried 2026-10-16: excluding them broke 6 corpus cases, Mushoku Tensei « II Part 2 » is Crunchyroll-only).
- **Season title before season number** (`bugfix/import-season-match`, 2026-10-15): Crunchyroll may count a special as a season (Dr. STONE « RYUSUI » = S3, so « Dr. STONE NEW WORLD » = S4 = the 3rd AniList group). `resolveByRules` stage `seasonByTitle` (`stages/season-by-title.ts`): a title matching entries of ONE season group places the episode in that group from the titled entry, later parts included (NEW WORLD E22 → Part 2 E11); if it does not fit there, a placement by number outside that group is `low`. A named season (own title, no number in it) placed by the raw platform rank ≥ 2 without signals carries `SyncTarget.byRank`: live sync still applies it, the history import sends it to « À vérifier ». Fractional episodes (24.5, OAD bonus) never reach the import (`parseHistoryItem` drops them) and resolve to `match.special` live; an OAD-titled season is special (`isSpecialSeason`), never a TV season. Corpus: `cr-import-drstone-*`, `cr-import-slime-*`.
- **Cached mappings questioned** (same branch): a cached mapping was reused forever (only signals questioned it), so a wrong one written by an applied import came back at every import and live sync. (1) Season title, live and import (`cachedTitleVerdict`, `resolver.ts` › `questionMapping`): an own-titled season (not the series title, no number, not special; `numbering: 'displayed'` and `chosen` skipped) whose cached entry does not carry the title and lies outside the group the title designates (`titleSeasonGroup`) → learned: dropped; unmarked: « À vérifier » (`titleMappingDoubt`); costs a by-id read plus the series searches (catalog cache) only when the cached entry lacks the title. (2) Import only, audit 1-D10 (`IMPORT_RESOLVE_OPTIONS.revalidateCache`, `revalidatedMapping`): every applicable non-chosen cached mapping is compared to a fresh resolution (searches pre-fetched in batches, `willSearch`); fresh `high` and another entry → learned: fresh result, and the plan mapping carries `replaces` so the apply step rewrites it (`saveLearned`, never over a choice made since); unmarked: « À vérifier » with the fresh entry proposed and the cached one selectable; fresh not high → cached kept. Live sync never re-resolves a cached mapping (corpus `cr-slime-s3e24-learned-mapping`, `ideal`). The mapping key is `crunchyroll:<series_id>:s<season_number>` (`season_number` of the history item, null → `s0`): two Crunchyroll seasons with the same `season_number` share one key (and one history row, `reduceHistory`), and seasons without one all share `s0`.
- **Series identity** (`platforms/series-identity.ts`, e2 `refactor/series-identity`, 2026-10-16, pure and content-safe): the only home of `seriesKey` (exclusions, ignored series), `mappingKey`, `seriesMappingPrefix` and the exclusion keys. Disney+ and Prime Video keys are ALWAYS title-based (`disney:title:{titleKey}`), even on a series page that knows the entity uuid / compact GTI (those ids only recognise AniList links), so a series page's « not an anime » verdict is shared with playback (M5). Exclusions (decision 7): a title-keyed series whose START year is known (`signals.seriesYear`, else the season year of season 1, never another season's year) is excluded under `…:title:{titleKey}:y{year}`; `exclusionKeyCovers` is cautious: same year, or either side without a year, matches (an old year-less exclusion keeps covering both remakes, and a series of unknown year is never synced when a homonym is excluded); two different years do not. Series with an id never carry a year. Arc mappings (Disney+ s99) are found from a series page showing that arc; the « furthest remembered season » fallback still ignores them (display only, no series → arc index).
- **Mapping store** (`browser/storage/mappings.ts`, e1 `refactor/mapping-store`, 2026-10-16): the only unconditional write is a user choice, `saveChosen` (season picked in the selector, validated « À vérifier » card); automatic writes go through `saveLearned(key, expected, mapping, origin = 'learned')` (compare-and-set, never over a `chosen` mapping unless the origin passed is `chosen`), automatic deletions through `forgetIfUnchanged`, « Oublier » through `forgetMapping`. `resolver.ts` › `resolveEpisode` reads it in three steps (e4): `fromCache` (imported mapping confirmed, questioned by signals / season title, import revalidation; served or « À vérifier »), `ignoredShortcut` (ignored series, no search), `fresh` (search, rules, `judge.ts`, `saveLearned`). The origin is read with `mappingOrigin()` and written with `withOrigin()` (`shared/sync.types.ts`), never through `.learned` / `.chosen` directly; the stored shape is unchanged (two optional flags, both together rejected by `isMediaMapping`, no migration). The service worker forgets its in-memory page resolutions after any mapping write (`onMappingsWritten`, in-process callbacks, not `storage.onChanged`) and on `MAPPINGS_CHANGED`, sent by the pages after « Oublier » and a backup restore (`ui/worker-call.ts` › `notifyMappingsChanged`, never throws).
- **Absolute numbering inside a Crunchyroll season** (Slime « Saison 2 » E25…E48, « Saison 3 » E49…E72, read 2026-10-15; history rows: `episodeNumber` = displayed 72, `seasonEpisodeNumber` = 24 from `relativeEpisodeNumber`, decimals 48.5 / 65.5 ignored): the cumulative split (stage `absoluteSplit`) starts from the first TV season, which must be among the candidates. AniList links Slime S2 to S1 only through the OVA « Coleus no Yume » (S2 PREQUEL → OVA → PREQUEL S1), and the relation walk only fetched TV/ONA nodes within `MAX_FETCH_ROUNDS` (2): S1 was missing, S3 E72 landed on S4 E24 (completed) and S2 E48 on S3. `candidateSteps`: with absolute numbering, once the ordinary rounds are spent, up to `MAX_PREQUEL_ROUNDS` (4) more rounds fetch the prequel chain only (series prequels, and any non-music PREQUEL of a series entry: `bridgesToEarlierSeason`), still skipped when a linked entry covers the number (One Piece). Guard: an absolute split landing on an entry titled as a LATER numbered season than the platform's (`titleSeasonOf` ≥ 2 and > platform season: a spill from missing prequels always goes forward) is `low`; a smaller title number is a platform counting a special as a season (Kaiju No. 8 « Season 2 » = Crunchyroll S3, E23 → 178754 E11, corpus `cr-import-kaiju-s3e23`), left as is; a series-titled entry (One Piece) is not concerned. Corpus: `cr-import-slime-s3e72`, `-s2e25-abs48`, `-s4e75-abs` (assumed).
- **AniList search and accents** (observed 2026-10-14): `search: "Moi, quand je me réincarne en Slime"` returns nothing although it is a synonym of entry 101280; the same text without accents finds it. Crunchyroll /fr/ series pages give the localized title (JSON-LD), sometimes after a first read of another source, so the card switched from found to « introuvable ». `collectCandidates` (`sync/candidates.ts`), when nothing linked is found, searches `withoutDiacritics(title)` then `slugSearchTitle(seriesSlug)` (English slug on Crunchyroll), exact queries deduplicated (`shared/title.ts`).
- **AniList catalog cache** (`background/anilist/media.ts` › `cachedPage`): search and by-id results cached 5 min (interactive) / 10 min (background), one request in flight per key; user lists are never cached there (display-only entries: see Page-media request budget). `collectCandidates` skips fetching missing sequels/prequels when the episode is numbered absolutely and a directly linked series entry covers it (airing or enough episodes: One Piece). Netflix One Piece page view or playback start: 2 searches (series + arc), no relation fetch.
- **Page-media request budget** (`bugfix/series-season-requests`, 2026-10-15: four season picks on the Attack on Titan Crunchyroll series page hit AniList's real 429, limit currently 30/min): `page-media.ts` › `prefetchSeasons` reads the card details of every picker season AND, signed in to AniList, their list entries in ONE authenticated `PageMediaBatch` (`anilist/page-batch.ts`, `Page(id_in)`: an alias per `Media(id:)` makes AniList answer 404 with every alias null as soon as one id is missing, seen 2026-10-15); public without AniList. Display-only list reads (`readEntry`: card lists, first-unfinished-season statuses) go through `trackers/entry-cache.ts` (2 min, keyed by `accountFingerprint`: epoch + raw stored token, so never served to another account; every tracker write forgets that service's entries, `forgettingEntries` in `trackers/index.ts`; reads that started before a write are not stored). Sync and actions still read `tracker.getEntry` fresh before writing. Failed batch: no retry for 5 s, single reads take over. Measured (`page-media/series-requests.test.ts`): first view 3 → 2 AniList requests, picks 3 / 3 / 1 / 7 → 1 / 1 / 0 / 1 (only the « series + season » search of a new season title remains, cached 5 min), plus one `PanelMedia` per newly shown entry (6 h cache, not batched: its relations make it heavy). The hourly airing check (`checkNewEpisodes('background')`, its stale-list refresh included) runs on the background lane; « Check now » stays interactive. Background lane budget (`rate-limit.ts`, PERF-21, decision of 2026-10-15): `backgroundQuota(X-RateLimit-Limit)` = limit − `INTERACTIVE_RESERVE` (10) − half of what exceeds 30, capped at 45 (90 → 45, 60 → 35), exactly `BACKGROUND_PER_MINUTE` (20) at 30 (AniList's degraded mode) or when unknown, recomputed on every observed limit and back to 20 after a 429; the dev trace's `network/request` events carry `backgroundQuota`. Job reads and writes (CR / ADN / Netflix import, list alignment, B9) reserve their background slot BEFORE the entry lock (`waitReadSlot` / `waitWriteSlot` → `reserveBackground`), then send `getEntry` / `saveProgress` / `saveEntry` with lane `'background'`, which consume the reserved slot without waiting (`PREPAID_SLOT_TTL_MS` 10 s, never during a 429 penalty): no interactive grace armed, no double count. Crunchyroll / ADN analysis (PERF-10): before each tranche of `PREFETCH_SEASONS` (30) seasons, `cr-import.ts` › `prefetchAhead` calls `resolver.ts` › `prefetchCandidates`, which runs the resolver's own search steps (`candidates.ts` › `candidateSteps`, a generator shared with `collectCandidates`: title, « series + season », season title, accent-free title, slug, then missing sequels / prequels) for all of them in lock-step, sends each step's searches through `searchAnimeBatch` and the missing ids by 50, then each season resolves from the 10 min background cache (seasons with an applicable cached mapping or an ignored series skipped; a failed prefetch is ignored). Measured (`history-import/cr-import-requests.test.ts`, same plans): 30 series 57 → 8 requests, 120 series 228 → 37; also after a resume (memory lost) and for the seasons recomputed by finalize.
- **Matching signals** (`feature/match-signals`, 2026-10-14): content scripts send `EpisodeInfo.signals` / `PageMediaInfo.signals` (`shared/match-signals.ts` › `PlatformSignals`: `seasonYear`, `seriesYear`, `seasonEpisodeCount`, `airing`, `episodeMinutes`, `format`, `originalTitle`, `country`; each optional, bounded by `toPlatformSignals`, no signal = old behaviour), read from verified fields only (never suggestions or user progress). AniList side: `duration`, `countryOfOrigin`, `status`, `endDate` in `MEDIA_FIELDS`, so a search costs complexity 35 (28 before): Netflix import batch of 10 = 350 of AniList's 500 (14 max).
  - concordance (`background/sync/matching/concordance.ts`, pure): season groups (`signal-groups.ts` › `signalGroups`: series entries linked or titled like the series plus their sequels / prequels in airing order, one franchise per connected chain, so two same-title remakes are two franchises; parts « Part 2 » grouped). Per group: year exact → agree, ±1 → weak (contradict if another group is exact), season starting during a long / airing group → unknown, else contradict; series year compared to the franchise's first group; episode count ±2 → agree, platform longer than a finished group by more than 2 per part → contradict (never with absolute numbering or airing; 86 EIGHTY-SIX 26 vs 11 + 12 stays unknown); duration ±30 %, < 8 vs ≥ 20 min → contradict; a platform movie contradicts a multi-episode series and vice versa; country only a weak hint; original title agree only. A contradicted format drops those candidates, a franchise with no concording group is dropped when another concords;
  - proof rule « prouvé ou à vérifier » (owner's decision): a title-only match (no AniList link to the platform) is `high` only when exactly one group survives and a proof signal (`PROOF_SIGNALS`: season or series year, episode count, original title) agrees with no weak contradiction; otherwise « À vérifier » with the reason (`match.signalsAgree` / `match.signalsContradict` / `match.titleOnly`). A group proven by the SEASON's year or count places the episode by its rank in the group (`provenTarget`, `bySignals`, treated as linked by `gateByPlatformLink`, `judge.ts`, inside the linked franchise only; decision 9, audit L1: proven by the episode count alone next to an entry excluded by an old slug, `other-slug`, it is capped to « À vérifier » in `finalize`, a season-year proof is kept): Mushoku Tensei Netflix S2 = « II » + « II Part 2 » (Part 2 linked to Crunchyroll only). Linked entries keep the old rules unless their group is contradicted;
  - cached mappings carry their origin: `learned: true` (saved by the resolution: contradicted by the signals → dropped and re-resolved), `chosen: true` (validated « À vérifier » card, season picked in the selector: never questioned), neither = unmarked (saved before 2.4.0 or restored from a backup: never deleted; contradicted → « À vérifier » `match.savedContradicted`, the proven entry proposed, the cached one still selectable). `contradictingSignals` checks format, season year beyond the airing span, series year > start + 1 and duration, never the episode count (the entry may be one part of the season). `revalidateCachedTarget` and backups keep both flags;
  - title keys (`mappingKey`, platforms without a series id: Disney+, Prime Video) gain `:y{seasonYear}` when the season year is known (`prime:title:berserk:y2016:s1`), so remakes never share a mapping; without the year the key is unchanged;
  - ADN: signals from the series page's JSON-LD only (`content/adn/adapter.ts` › `adnSeriesSignals`: `@type` TVSeries / Movie → format, `datePublished` → series start year, `alternativeHeadline` → original title « 桃源暗鬼 », `countryOfOrigin.name` « Japon » → JP, `numberOfEpisodes` only when `containsSeason` holds a single season); the watch page's `partOfSeries` only has the name, so ADN playback sends none;
  - corpus `src/background/sync/__corpus__` (README there): real cases run through the real resolver against AniList snapshots (`anilist/*.json`), `expected` asserted strictly, `ideal` + `gap` reported as todos; refresh the snapshots with `node scripts/refresh-anilist-corpus.ts [--pending] [franchise]` (network, anonymous, outside `verify`; its `MEDIA_FIELDS` copy is checked against `background/anilist/media.ts`);
  - diagnostic in dev builds only (`npm run build:dev`): `resolveEpisode` records each decision under `if (__SYNCKAI_DEBUG__)` (`background/sync/match-diagnostic.ts` › `recordMatchDecision`): platform, mapping key, titles, season / episode numbers, signals, up to 12 candidates (id, title, start year, episodes, format, duration, link, kept, per-signal check of their group) and the decision (source cache / doubted / resolved / ignored, mediaId, confidence, reason, `bySignals`, cached mapping origin learned / chosen / unmarked and its verdict keep / drop / doubt). Last 50 in `chrome.storage.session` under `MATCH_DIAGNOSTIC_KEY` (`debug:matchDiagnostic`, a separate export of `protocol/storage-keys.ts` so it leaves published bundles), never `local`. *Settings › Help & about* shows a « Matching diagnostic » section in dev builds (`ui/settings/match-diagnostic.tsx`, rendered under `__SYNCKAI_DEBUG__` in `page-help.tsx`, module without top-level side effect so the published bundles drop it): collapsed by default (header = title, count and chevron: `Collapsible` `variant="section"`, open state kept while the page is shown, like the debug trace), newest first, « Copy » = one decision as JSON (paste it to make a corpus case), « Copy all » = every decision as one JSON array, « Clear ». Check after a change: `npm run build` then `grep -rl debug:matchDiagnostic dist` finds nothing (the `settings.matchDiag.*` texts stay in the English catalog, as data).
