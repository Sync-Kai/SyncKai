# SyncKai term sheet: Spanish (Latin America), `es-419`

Binding for every change to `src/i18n/locales/es-419.json` and `public/_locales/es_419/messages.json`. Shared rules: `docs/i18n/GLOSSARY.md`.

Target: neutral Latin American Spanish as used by Crunchyroll Latin America and the big apps sold in Mexico, Argentina, Colombia and Chile. It is a separate locale from Spain Spanish (`es`), not a copy: vocabulary, quotation marks, percent format and verb tenses differ on purpose (see section 3).

## 1. Core concepts (GLOSSARY.md section 3)

| English term | es-419 | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| series | **serie** (fem.), plural **series** | Neutral word for an anime title. Every participle or adjective about a series is feminine: *serie excluida*, *{title}: completada*, *Califícala en Actividad*. |
| anime | **anime** (masc.), plural **animes** | LatAm fans pluralize it (*mis animes*, *solo se sincronizan los animes vinculados…*). |
| season / episode / arc | **temporada** / **episodio** / **arco** | Standard. |
| Ep. (before a number) | **Ep.** / **ep.**; plural-aware count: **{count} ep.** / **{count} eps.** | Usual fandom abbreviation. Non-plural-aware counts (`review.episodes`) keep **ep.**, valid for any number. |
| list | **lista** (*tu lista*, *tus listas*) | The user's tracking list, never a playlist. |
| entry | **ficha** (fem.) | The anime's page on AniList/MyAnimeList (*Abrir la ficha en AniList*, *Ficha única*). Not *entrada* (also means a blog post) nor *registro*. |
| Watching (status and popup tab) | **Viendo** | Short, universal, fits the tab. |
| Completed | **Completado** | Status names are masculine labels, as on Spanish-language trackers. A participle about a series agrees with *serie*: *{title}: completada*, *(completada)*. |
| On hold (AniList Paused / MAL On-Hold) | **En pausa** | Clear and mirrors AniList. Actions: **Pausar** (compact button), **Poner en pausa** (menu). |
| Dropped | **Abandonado** | Standard fandom term. Action: **Abandonar**; confirmation **¿Abandonarla?** (never reads as *Cancelar*). |
| Plan to watch (AniList Planning) | **Por ver** | Natural and short. *Pendiente* is avoided because it collides with pending syncs. In buttons the name is quoted: *Agregar a “Por ver”*. |
| Rewatching (AniList REPEATING) | **Reviendo** | *rever* = to watch again, natural in LatAm. *Repitiendo* (literal) sounds wrong. |
| rewatch (noun) | **volver a ver** (phrase), or the status name **Reviendo** | No natural LatAm noun (*revisionado* is Spain-only, *rewatch* is an anglicism). Phrasing: *No se pudo marcar como Reviendo*, *propuesta de volver a ver rechazada*. |
| Activity (tab) | **Actividad** | |
| sync (noun) / to sync | **sincronización** / **sincronizar**; **sincronizado**, **Sincronizando…** | One noun and one verb everywhere. No *sincro* or *sync* abbreviations. |
| to check (`review.*`, `footer.toReview`) | **por revisar**; a to-check card = **revisión** (*Crear 3 revisiones*) | Natural LatAm wording. Any other "check" (check now, check the tab, check your connection) also uses **revisar**, as English reuses "check". |
| to rate | **por calificar** | |
| score / rating / stars | **calificación**; verb **calificar** | LatAm word (Netflix, Google Play). *valoración*/*puntuación*/*nota* are Spain-leaning. AniList average: *Calificación promedio en AniList* (*promedio*, not *media*). |
| comment (`notes.*`) | **comentario** | Matches MyAnimeList "Comments". Never *nota* (that means a grade). |
| match, mapping (Settings) | **correspondencia** (*Correspondencias*, *{count} correspondencias*) | A remembered season → AniList entry link. |
| match to (AniList ↔ MAL alignment, `compare.*`) | verb **igualar** (*Igualar todo a AniList*); the running job = **actualización** | Different concept from matches. *Igualar a* is short and clear. Job banners: *Actualización terminada / detenida / interrumpida*. |
| excluded series | **serie excluida**; chip **Excluida**; section **Series excluidas** | Action button: **Dejar de sincronizar**; undo: **Reactivar**, **Reanudar sincronización**. |
| pending sync, queue | **sincronización pendiente**; Activity heading **Pendientes ({count})** | The heading is shortened, as in German, to fit the 25-character hint. Discard a queued sync = **Descartar**, not *Abandonar*, which is the Dropped status. |
| credits | **créditos** (*al inicio de los créditos*) | Crunchyroll/Netflix LatAm wording. *ending* is the song, not the credits. |
| popup | **ventana emergente** | Usual LatAm term (Chrome es-419). |
| side panel | **panel lateral** | Chrome es-419 term. Also used for Firefox (where it is the sidebar). |
| Now playing (panel tab) | **Reproduciendo** | 13 characters against the ≤ 12 hint; it fits the measured space and every shorter option was unnatural or collided with *Viendo*. |
| Schedule (panel tab, `agenda.*`) | **Calendario** | LatAm fans say *calendario de estrenos*. Reused in *Avisos y calendario*. |
| On this page | **En esta página** | |
| preferred player | **plataforma preferida** | The context note says "player" means the streaming service. *reproductor* is kept for the video player (*Esperando el reproductor…*). |
| history import | **importar historial**; noun **importación**; title **Importar historial** | Watch history = **historial de reproducción**. Netflix's own page is **Actividad de visualización**. |
| backup | **respaldo** (*Respaldo · SyncKai*, *synckai-respaldo-{date}.json*) | Usual LatAm word, short, never used for the history import. *copia de seguridad* is the Spain-leaning form. |
| preview | **vista previa** | |
| account / connect / sign in / sign out / session expired | **cuenta** / **conectar** / **iniciar sesión** / **cerrar sesión**, **desconectar** / **sesión expirada** | *expirar*, not *caducar* (Spain). AniList/MAL "sign-ins" stored in backups = **sesiones**. |
| release alerts | **alertas de estreno** | *estreno* is the LatAm word for a new release. |
| in-page "alerts only" level | **Solo advertencias** (*advertencias*) | Must differ from release alerts: there it means warnings that need action. |
| notifications (umbrella: settings category and group) | **avisos** (*Avisos y calendario*, *Reproducción y avisos*) | Short umbrella word. The section itself says *Notificaciones en la página*. |
| release / airing | **estreno** (on a platform) / **emisión** (TV broadcast in Japan; *En emisión*) | *emisión en Japón* = Japanese broadcast. |
| airing statuses | **En emisión**, **Finalizado**, **Cancelado**, **En hiato**, **Próximamente** | Masculine (they describe the anime). *Finalizado* ≠ the user's *Completado*. *En hiato* ≠ the user's *En pausa*. |
| offset / delay | **desfase** (episode-number offset) / **retraso** (Japanese broadcast → platform) | |
| shortcut | **atajo de teclado**, **atajo** | Firefox and the colloquial LatAm term. |
| Settings | **Configuración** | LatAm OS/app term. *Ajustes* is the Spain/iOS-ES term. |
| Settings categories | **Ver y sincronizar** · **Avisos y calendario** · **Atajos de teclado** · **Mis datos** · **Ayuda e información** | Each fits the ≤ 20–24 character hint. *Reproducción y sincronización* (29) would be cut off in the top bar. |
| Settings groups | **Configuración rápida** · **Reproducción y avisos** · **Datos y ayuda** | |
| Settings sections | Cuentas, Idioma, Acerca de, Ayuda, Respaldo, Importar, Series excluidas, Correspondencias, Zona de peligro, Reproducción, Sincronización, Panel lateral, Calendario, Nuevos episodios, Notificaciones en la página | |

## 3. Conventions

- **Register**: *tú* everywhere (*Conecta*, *Inténtalo*, *tu lista*). No *voseo* (*conectá*, *probá*), no *vosotros*, no *usted*.
- **Tenses**: recent events use the simple preterite, as in Latin America (*No se encontró*, *No se pudo*, *Se guardó*, *La sesión expiró*, *No iniciaste sesión*). Never the Spain-style *no se ha encontrado*.
- **Vocabulary to avoid** (Spain-only): ordenador, vale, coger, móvil (→ celular), vídeo, icono, pulsar (→ hacer clic/presionar), fichero, ajustes, valorar/valoración, nota (for a score), cuenta atrás, en directo, media (→ promedio), gestionar, añadir, introducir (→ ingresar), copia de seguridad (→ respaldo), Mayús. For software that does not answer, write *conectar con* (*No se pudo conectar con la extensión*), not *contactar con*.
- **Countdowns and retries**: never use a gerund for a future event. *reanudando en 42 s* and *reintentando en 30 s* copy the English; write *se reanuda en {seconds} s* and *nuevo intento en {seconds} s*. A gerund for an action in progress is fine (*Sincronizando…*, *Reanudando después de una interrupción…*).
- **Settings paths in sentences**: do not echo the word *Configuración* (*se configuran en Configuración › …*). Use another verb (*Activa… y elige… en {path}*).
- **Quotation marks**: English-style curly double quotes **“ ”** (CLDR es-419 and LatAm usage). Spain uses « ».
- **Questions and exclamations**: always opened (*¿Tu calificación?*, *¡Completaste “{title}”!*).
- **Colon**: no space before it (*{label}: {value}*). The text after it starts lowercase unless it is a full sentence or a UI name.
- **Ellipsis**: the single character **…** (*Sincronizando…*).
- **Separators**: ` › ` in settings paths and ` · ` between items, exactly as in English.
- **Abbreviations**: **min** (minutes), **h** (hours), **d** (days), **s** (seconds), **MB**, **Ep./ep./eps.**, **JP** (Japan). They are not pluralized.
- **Percent**: no space, **85%** (CLDR es-419). Spain writes *85 %*.
- **Relative times**: *hace 5 min*, *hace 2 h*, *hace 3 días*, *ayer*, *ahora mismo*. They read naturally after *Analizado*, *Completada* and *Última revisión:*. *ahora mismo* is preferred over *hace un momento* because it is shorter in the footer (*Todo sincronizado · ahora mismo*).
- **Counters without plural variants** (`{done} / {total}`, `{pages}`, `{rows}`): put the noun in a label, *Análisis de temporadas: 12 / 48*, *(filas: 240)*, so that a total of 1 stays grammatical.
- **Composed texts**:
  - `{service}: {message}` → *AniList: episodio 5 guardado*. Fragments start lowercase and have no final period.
  - `{title}: completed` → *{title}: completada* (also *abandonada*, *en pausa*, *eliminada de tu lista*). Agrees with *serie*, not with the title.
  - `Next try {when}` → *Próximo intento en 5 min* / *en breve* (the preposition is inside {when}).
  - `{platforms}` lists come from Intl.ListFormat (*Crunchyroll, ADN o Netflix*). `panel.offTargetEnable` is written *Para activar {platforms}: {path}.* to avoid singular/plural agreement.
- **Plurals**: `_one` (exactly 1) and `_other` (0, 2, 3, 21, 101…). `_many` (only for millions) is not provided and falls back to `_other`.
- **Capitalization**: sentence case. List status names keep their capital inside sentences (*cuando una serie pasa a Completado*).

## 4. Netflix

- "Show more" button at the bottom of netflix.com/viewingactivity: **Mostrar más**. Source: Netflix's Latin American Spanish help center (help.netflix.com/es/node/101917): “Si ves una lista reducida, presiona el botón Mostrar más.” The use of *presiona* confirms the LatAm variant. Confidence: high (official documentation), not checked on a live LatAm account.
- "Viewing activity" page name: **Actividad de visualización** (same help article, and LatAm press articles).
